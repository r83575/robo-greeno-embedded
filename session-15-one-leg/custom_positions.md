# Custom Leg Positions

## Description

Additional custom positions for the Session 15 hexapod one-leg control project.

---

## Positions

| Position | Hip | Femur | Tibia | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| CROUCH | 90 | 110 | 140 | Low bent position |
| HIGH_LIFT | 90 | 20 | 80 | Higher foot lift |
| FORWARD_LOW | 130 | 80 | 130 | Forward low placement |
